Dr. Yvan I. Russell

About Me

Dr. Yvan I. Russell is an interdisciplinary cognitive scientist whose research spans evolutionary psychology, primatology, behavioural economics, and the cognitive science of religion. His work examines how reputation, reciprocity, and cooperation shape social behaviour — in great apes, in humans playing economic games, and, more recently, in large language models.

He has published across an unusually wide range of fields, reflecting a career built on cross-disciplinary collaboration. He has held academic posts at Oxford, Brunel, Göttingen, and Middlesex University, where he founded and taught the university’s first primatology module. He is based in Buckinghamshire, United Kingdom.

In 2024, Dr. Russell stepped back from his position at Middlesex University to focus full-time on research. After more than a decade of heavy administrative and teaching commitments, he is now reinventing his academic identity — returning to the questions that first drew him to science and pursuing new collaborations, several of which have already led to publications in 2025.
